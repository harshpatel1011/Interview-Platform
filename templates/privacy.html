Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ now|date:"F j, Y"|default:"September 22, 2026" }}

1. Information We Collect

At TechPlus, we collect information that you provide directly to us when you register for an account, participate in technical interviews, or communicate with our support team. This includes:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, role (Candidate, Interviewer, or Management), and encrypted passwords.
  • Profile Data: Resumes, CVs, department assignments, and job application history.
  • Interview Content: Code written during coding assessments, audio and video recordings (if opted-in), chat transcripts, and evaluation scores or feedback.
  • Usage Data: IP addresses, browser types, log data, and interaction metrics on our platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We strictly use the collected data to facilitate and improve the hiring process. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Create and manage your user account.
  • Schedule and host secure video conferencing rooms via Jitsi integration.
  • Share candidate profiles, resumes, and interview feedback with authorized Management and Interviewer personnel.
  • Analyze platform performance and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Send automated email notifications regarding upcoming interviews or account changes.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information in the following circumstances:

  • With Employers/Management: If you are a candidate, your profile, resume, and interview results are shared with the hiring company managing the pipeline.
  • Service Providers: We use trusted third-party services (like AWS for hosting and Jitsi for video) who are strictly bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, subpoena, or other legal processes.

4. Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures designed to protect your personal information. Video calls are encrypted in transit. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 algorithms. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you may have the following rights:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to rectify inaccurate data.
  • The right to erase your data (the "Right to be Forgotten") using the Account Deletion tool in your dashboard.
  • The right to restrict or object to our processing of your data.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@techplus.io.
